About the Online Doctor Appointment Booking System

The Doctor Appointment System is a modern web application designed to simplify and digitize the process of booking, managing, and tracking medical appointments for hospitals and clinics.

Key Features:

  • For Patients: Register and log in, search for doctors by department, view doctor profiles, book appointments based on real-time availability, view and manage appointment history, access medical records, rate and review doctors, and manage personal account details.
  • For Doctors: Log in securely, set and update their availability, view and manage upcoming appointments, approve or cancel appointments, and update their profile and specialization.
  • For Admins: Manage doctor and patient accounts, monitor all appointments, and access analytics dashboards for trends and user activity.

Why use our system?
  • 24/7 online appointment booking and management
  • Reduces waiting time and administrative workload
  • Ensures secure and private handling of patient data
  • Improves communication between patients, doctors, and staff
  • Mobile-friendly and easy to use for all users

Our goal is to make healthcare more accessible, efficient, and transparent for everyone. Whether you are a patient seeking care, a doctor managing your schedule, or an admin overseeing operations, our system is built to support your needs.
