In the modern world, the use of digital technology in healthcare has become essential. Patients often 
struggle with traditional appointment systems, where booking an appointment means visiting a clinic, 
waiting in long queues, or making phone calls that may go unanswered. These outdated processes 
waste both time and resources.
To overcome these challenges, this project titled “Online Doctor Appointment Booking System”
has been developed. It is a web-based platform that enables patients to book appointments with 
doctors easily and efficiently from any location. This system bridges the gap between patients and 
healthcare providers by offering an organized, time-saving, and user-friendly approach to scheduling 
consultations.
This project is designed for three types of users:
• Patients:- who can register, search for doctors, and book or cancel appointments.
• Doctors:-who can manage their availability, view patient bookings, and update time slots.
• Admin:- who oversee the overall functioning of the system and manage doctors and patients.
The platform is built using HTML, CSS and JS for frontend design, PHP for backend functionality, 
and MySQL for secure data storage. It aims to simplify healthcare access, reduce manual workload, 
and improve appointment management in clinics and hospitals.
By integrating digital solutions in healthcare appointment systems, this project is a step toward 
smarter healthcare services and better patient-doctor coordination

OBJECTIVES
1. To simplify and digitize the process of scheduling medical consultations by providing an 
easy-to-access web platform for patients and doctors.
2. To minimize patient wait times and reduce manual appointment handling, thus enhancing 
the efficiency of clinics and hospitals.
3. To ensure secure and structured management of user data, appointments, and feedback 
through an integrated database system.
4. To provide role-based access and functionality for patients, doctors, and administrators 
with real-time updates and control.
5. To enhance patient-doctor communication and trust through a user-friendly interface and 
feedback mechanism.
6. To eliminate geographical and time-related barriers by offering an online, accessibleanytime platform for healthcare appointment booking.
7. To support future expansion such as video consultation, online prescriptions, and payment 
integration using scalable technologies
